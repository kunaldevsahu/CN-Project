## What mac3 sent to the INTERNET meanwhile (only team1.test names shown; capture evidence/phase2/A/08-mac3-upstream.pcap)
   15:47:10.623036 IP 10.7.3.175.51804 > 8.8.8.8.53: 40788+ [1au] A? probe-1795601825.team1.test. (56)

Reading: NXDOMAIN / empty answer with NO authority section = dnsmasq answered from its own zone (local=/team1.test/), nothing left the LAN (mac1).
Authority = the ROOT zone's SOA (a.root-servers.net) = the question was forwarded to the internet (8.8.8.8, in the capture): internal names leak and the answer is slower (mac3).
Fix: add local=/team1.test/ (and no-hosts) to mac3's dnsmasq.conf, as in dns/phase2/dnsmasq.conf.
